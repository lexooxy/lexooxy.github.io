Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shorts Autopilot (“the app”) handles data. The app is operated by Lexo (“we”), contact: orbitwhatifs@gmx.net. It is a private tool used by its operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channel(s).

1. YouTube API Services

Shorts Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data that Google and YouTube process.

2. What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant access, the app can access the following data of your own YouTube channel:

Permission (scope)What it is used for
youtube.uploadUploading videos you approved in the app to your channel, as scheduled videos with the publish time you chose.
youtubeReading your channel name and video list (to show your videos and avoid duplicate uploads), setting the thumbnail and title of your own videos, and reading recent comments on your own videos to show them in the app.
yt-analytics.readonlyReading statistics of your own channel (views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained) to show charts in the app.

The app also receives an OAuth access token and refresh token from Google so it can act on your behalf.

3. How the data is used

  • Only to provide the features you see in the app: scheduling uploads, showing your videos, statistics and comments.
  • Statistics may be summarised (for example “videos about danger to Earth get more views”) to suggest topics for your next videos. This summary is shown only to you.
  • The app never publishes a video immediately, never deletes videos and never posts or answers comments.
  • The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the service providers listed in section 5, which are needed to run the app.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are transferred only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and kept in a private storage area that only the app's worker can read. They are never shown in the app, in messages or in logs.
  • Channel statistics, video titles and comments are stored in the app's private database, visible only to the signed-in owner.
  • Statistics from the YouTube API are refreshed regularly and are not kept longer than 30 days without being refreshed or deleted, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

5. Service providers

To run the app we use these providers, which process data only on our behalf:

  • Google / YouTube – sign-in, video upload and statistics (privacy policy).
  • Anthropic (Claude) – hosting of the app and the automated worker that prepares videos and reads statistics (privacy policy).
  • ElevenLabs – text-to-speech for video narration; receives only video scripts, no Google user data (privacy policy).

6. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working immediately. Stored statistics and comments of a channel that stays disconnected are deleted after 30 days at the latest.
  • You can ask us to delete all stored data by writing to orbitwhatifs@gmx.net. We delete it within 7 days.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking. The app stores small settings (such as the selected channel) in your browser.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at orbitwhatifs@gmx.net.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
