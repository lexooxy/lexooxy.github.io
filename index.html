Plan, schedule and understand your own YouTube Shorts.

Shorts Autopilot is a small private tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel OrbitWhatIfs (science and space “what if” Shorts). It helps the channel owner prepare videos, schedule them on their own channel and read their own channel statistics in one place.

Schedule uploads

The owner approves a finished video in the app. The tool uploads it to the owner's channel as a scheduled video at the time the owner chose.

Own channel statistics

Views, watch time and retention of the owner's videos are shown as charts, so the owner can see which topics work.

Comment overview

Recent comments on the owner's videos are listed in one place. The tool never posts or answers comments on its own.

How it works

  1. The channel owner signs in with Google and grants access to their own YouTube channel.
  2. The owner reviews each prepared video (preview, title, description) and approves or rejects it.
  3. Approved videos are uploaded to the owner's channel with the owner's chosen publish date.
  4. Statistics from YouTube Analytics are shown back to the owner in the app.

Who can use it

Shorts Autopilot is not a public service. It is used only by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channel(s). There is no sign-up, no advertising and no sale of data.

Google and YouTube data

The app requests access to the owner's YouTube account only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to read the owner's own channel statistics. Details are in the Privacy Policy. By using Shorts Autopilot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Shorts Autopilot Terms of Service.

You can remove the app's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party access page.
